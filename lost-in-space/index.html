How to play

  • Read the mission card on the right. It tells you what to fix, with a checklist.
  • Type commands in the terminal and press Enter. There's more than one right way — the ship checks what you changed, not exactly what you typed.
  • Stuck? Press Hint. Each press gets more specific: a story hint, then the idea, then the command, then a worked example.
  • Made a mess? Press Try again to rewind the mission. If you delete something important, D.U.S.T. restores it automatically.
  • Handy keys: Tab finishes names, ↑ brings back old commands, Ctrl+L clears the screen.
  • Click rooms on the ship to find out which folder they live in.
  • The terminal is a faithful simulation of a Linux shell that runs entirely in your browser — nothing here can touch the files on your real computer.
